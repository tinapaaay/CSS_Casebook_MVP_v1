How an investigation works

Don’t memorize a fix.
Follow the evidence.

  1. 01

    Inspect

    Read the incident report and compare the broken interface with its target.

  2. 02

    Experiment

    Edit the CSS and observe the rendered result as your hypothesis changes.

  3. 03

    Resolve

    Check the visible outcome, then study the root cause in the resolution report.
